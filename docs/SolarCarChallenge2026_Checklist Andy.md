## Andy notes:

Telemetry systems may have a common ground with the propulsion system, as long as the current draw is low

Liquid Container – Cockpits must be equipped with a plastic liquid container filled with water, which can be easily reached by the driver. The liquid container must be secured from movement such that it cannot roll underneath the brake and throttle controls

Fire Extinguishers – Cockpits must be equipped with a Class C fire extinguisher which can be easily reached by the driver in the event of an emergency. Chase vehicles must also be equipped with either a Class C fire extinguisher (for lead-acid batteries), a Class D fire extinguisher (for Lithium-metal batteries), or an appropriate fire extinguisher for other battery chemistries.
